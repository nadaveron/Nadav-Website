Nadav Friedman

Percussionist · Composer · Bandleader
Traditions in Rhythm

Israeli percussionist, composer and bandleader specializing in Middle Eastern percussion and drum set. He plays a setup of his own that joins darbuka, frame drums and riq with the modern kit, moving between the rhythms of the Middle East and North Africa, the Balkans, Persia and Latin America. He has recorded on more than 15 albums.
