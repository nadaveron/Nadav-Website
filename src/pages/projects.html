Balkan & Gypsy band · Percussion & management

Bombaras

A high-energy Balkan-Gypsy band with Eastern European roots. Bombaras joins Gypsy and Balkan traditions with Eastern European Jewish music, songs of wandering, freedom, heartache and joy, and plays them as a live haflah built for dancing.

Bombaras' members play in international Gypsy groups such as The Turbans and Gute Gute. The group has performed at major venues across Israel and is currently recording its first studio album.

Alma de Bata

An original production with percussion at the centre of the stage. Three percussionists each bring a tradition, Nadav on Middle Eastern drums, Rony Iwryn on Latin percussion and Itamar Gutman on West African rhythms, joined by Jewish and Arab musicians in music composed for the project.

Supported by FeelBeit and Mifal HaPais, Alma de Bata has received grants for innovation and invitations to perform at the Israel Festival and other festivals.

Mirror

Four musicians, two Palestinian and two Jewish. Each brings the music of their own home, the group then plays it through each other's musical language, and the evening ends with music born from the meeting, including Nadav's new compositions Mirror I and Mirror II.

Songs for Empathy

An album built on one idea: empathy, seeing the other person as part of yourself. Nadav's compositions draw on jazz, Latin, classical and Middle Eastern music, and grew out of experiences of immigration, homelessness, war and loss, from the streets of Boston to social activists in Israel.
