About

A percussionist of many traditions

Nadav Friedman is an Israeli percussionist, composer and educator. He creates original productions for FeelBeit in Jerusalem, among them Alma de Bata and Mirror, leads the Balkan-Gypsy band Bombaras, co-leads Playground Trio, and performs with artists including Itai Armon, Shimon Buskila and Rotem Cohen.

His music took shape in Jerusalem, where he was a founding member of the Andalusian orchestra HaMadrasa and co-founded the Soltz Trio. He went on to a Master's degree at the New England Conservatory in Boston, performing and studying with Danilo Pérez, Dave Holland and Jason Moran, and returned to Israel in 2023 with his debut album, Songs for Empathy. Today he teaches at the HaSadna and Kiryat Ono conservatories and in his studio in Neve Shalom, and gives masterclasses, most recently at Bar-Ilan University.
